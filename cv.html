Enterprise NFR Work Fabric | Case study
Private blueprint and interactive experience, sanitised

Enterprise AI Economics Simulator | Case study
Interactive decision tool, AI-assisted prototype
  • Executive cost model for a shared AI platform: allocation across the functions sharing it, maturity, hosting, governance staffing, licences and usage, with a ten-year cash flow and break-even.
  • Commercial relevance: At its illustrative defaults, fixed platform and governance costs are about 96 percent of year-one cost and tokens under 1 percent, which moves the business case from token prices to sharing, maturity and hosting.
  • Illustrative planning assumptions and placeholder benchmarks. Not a client tool, a proposal or a production system.
DORA Article 12 AI Control Line | Case study
Working workflow prototype, AI-assisted
  • Takes one regulatory obligation through guideline, control, infrastructure proposal, policy verification, documentation and evidence, with an explicit state machine, three human approval gates and hash-linked provenance.
  • Commercial relevance: Shows how regulatory change can stay traceable and human-approved end to end, with task-based model assignment and deterministic verification before review.
  • Synthetic fixtures; cloud, deployment and register integrations simulated; client context excluded.
